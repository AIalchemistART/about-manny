### SCMS and Mneme
I created SCMS, an open-source framework for making AI-assisted work repeatable:
useful patterns are captured, promoted when they keep proving useful, and retired
when they don't. It ships as a starter kit, whitepapers and an SSRN preprint
(5656271). Its approach was independently validated by findings in Google's Nested
Learning research (NeurIPS 2025). Google did not review SCMS; its findings support
the same approach in a different domain. I built Mneme on it: a chat app whose
long-term memory users can inspect and correct, plus an MCP server so other AI
tools share the same memory.
Links: https://github.com/AIalchemistART/scms-starter-kit ·
https://mneme-ai.netlify.app

### Clarity Intelligence Systems
A private AI family-history assistant, first for my own family and now offered as

answer. It runs locally on the client's computer after delivery, with a
citations-only Research mode.
Link: https://clarityintelligence.io
